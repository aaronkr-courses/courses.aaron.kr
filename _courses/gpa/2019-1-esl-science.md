Improving English through **science**. Students progressed through *The ESL Science Book*, which combines reading, writing, critical thinking, and activities, along with video lessons, report writing, and quizzes and tests.

**A consistent weekly rhythm** for each chapter:

- *Before day 1:* preview the next reading, highlight unknown words, and find their Korean translations
- *Day 1:* watch a short video introduction and discuss it (including new vocabulary), then read together
- *Day 2:* re-read as review and complete the comprehension questions together — with grammatically correct example sentences on the board
- *Homework:* finish any activities and preview the next chapter

Every two chapters ended in a review lesson and writing assignment; every five chapters, a project.

**Chapters:** dinosaurs · global warming · stem cells · killer bees · Alzheimer's disease · dentistry · the Internet · 2003 UB313 · acupuncture · earthquakes · identical twins · lasers · astrology · xerography · carnivorous plants · automation · the lunar landing · DNA · sports medicine · the Hubble Space Telescope · genetic engineering · endangered species · hydroponics · satellites · rain forests.
