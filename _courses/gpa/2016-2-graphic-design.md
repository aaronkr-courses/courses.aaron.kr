A foundation in the basics of **graphic design**, taught through a semester-long **rebranding campaign**. This elective introduces students to real-world design problems and solutions that designers and companies face every day when marketing themselves and their products.

By the end of the course, students will demonstrate:

- Knowledge of the basic components of graphic design: color, font choice and pairing, layout and whitespace, balance, and taking a publication from concept to print
- A practical understanding of branding and how companies use "brand power" in marketing campaigns
- Functional knowledge of Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign
- A design portfolio — and a **brand book** for a rebranded product, service, or company

**Section I — Design:** Canva challenges and design concepts (fonts, colors, layout, images), plus the school's monthly newsletters.

**Section II — Branding:** students choose a struggling or outdated brand and work through brand research, SWOT and competitive analysis, a creative brief and taglines, logo design in Illustrator, a creative toolkit, print ads in InDesign, brand "SWAG" in Photoshop, and finally the brand book.

**Materials:** students keep an online design notebook (keywords, design questions, sketches, research) in Google Drive; tutorials from Canva and Lynda.com.
