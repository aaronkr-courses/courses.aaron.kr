The College Board **AP Computer Science Principles** course, taught with the [Code.org CSP](https://code.org/educate/csp) curriculum:

- **Unit 1 — The Internet:** personal innovations, binary and number systems, sending numbers and text, addressing, redundancy and reliability, DNS and HTTP
- **Unit 2 — Digital Information:** file sizes and compression, encoding images, lossy vs. lossless formats
- **Unit 4 — Big Data and Privacy:** trends, research, people and data, encryption, cybercrime
- **Explore performance task** (eight class days)
- **Unit 3 — Algorithms and Programming** and **Unit 5 — Building Apps** in App Lab: Clicker Game, Color Sleuth, Image Scroller, Canvas Painter
- **Create performance task** through the end of the semester
