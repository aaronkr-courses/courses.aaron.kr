The second semester of Code.org [**Computer Science Discoveries**](https://code.org/educate/csd):

- **Unit 4 — The Design Process:** analysis of design, understanding users, user interfaces, paper prototypes, App Lab, and app presentations
- **Unit 5 — Data and Society:** representing information (binary, ASCII, images, numbers), keeping data secret, problem solving with big data, structuring and interpreting data
- **Unit 6 — Physical Computing:** programming the Circuit Playground — board events, analog input, arrays and color LEDs, making music, the accelerometer, and prototyping an innovation
