Graphic design through **branding**. In the surviving half of the semester, students studied logo design (color and font guides, brand language, touchpoints, brand management, loyalty and validity), produced **100 logo sketches**, refined three into **Illustrator logos**, and applied the brand across a creative toolkit, testimonial and print ads, a newsletter, website design, and "SWAG" — finishing with a **rebrand book**.
