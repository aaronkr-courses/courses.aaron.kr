The goal of this course is a solid foundation of computer knowledge — **computer literacy** — for every student. This middle-school class covers the same material as **Computer A** (a required high school class), and the concepts covered directly impact students' success in high school, at university and, later, in the job market.

By the end of the course, students will demonstrate:

- Knowledge of the basic components and peripherals of computers
- A deep, practical understanding of online safety and computer security (social networking, program installation, virus detection & removal, passwords)
- Functional knowledge of Google Drive and the Google tools (Gmail, Search, Calendar, Maps, Docs, Sheets, Slides)
- Functional knowledge of the major office suites, online and offline
- A computer portfolio showcasing their skills and projects

**Section I — Knowledge:** touch typing · computer basics (with [GCF LearnFree](https://edu.gcfglobal.org/en/computerbasics/)) · Google tools · office suites. Graded mainly with worksheets and quizzes.

**Section II — Application** (after midterms): students (1) write a research paper in APA style, (2) build calendars, schedules, and budgets in Sheets, (3) design a presentation and deliver it as a speech, and (4) design a PowerPoint game.

**Materials:** no textbook — worksheets and handouts are provided and shared through Google Drive. Fridays are catch-up & portfolio days.
