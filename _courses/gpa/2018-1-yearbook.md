Yearbook production — **writing, photography, and design**. Students started with Canva design challenges and a school pennant, then studied the creative process, layout and composition (grids, layout analysis), typography, and color, with tours of **Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign**. A 5-day photo challenge (composition, landscapes, street photography, portraiture) and article writing led into choosing the yearbook theme and cover, building InDesign templates, and laying out the yearbook pages.
