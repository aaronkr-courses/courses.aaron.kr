Computer A, rebuilt around the **CompTIA A+** certification — the entry-level IT credential most tech companies recognize, and a launching pad to more advanced certifications.

After a short touch-typing unit, students worked through the two A+ exams objective by objective:

- **220-901** — hardware (CPUs, RAM, BIOS, motherboards, power supplies, hard drives, peripherals, building a PC), displays and printers, networking (LANs, wireless, the Internet), and laptops & mobile devices
- **220-902** — operating systems (Windows, OS X, Linux), users and permissions, maintenance, the command line, troubleshooting, virtualization, mobile devices, and security

Each topic block ended with a quiz/test, and the semester closed with a Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides refresher.
