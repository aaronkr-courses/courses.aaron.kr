Being safe, responsible, and respectful online, using the **Common Sense Media** Digital Citizenship curriculum. After the *Digital Compass* game, surveys, and Lynda.com's digital citizenship course, students worked through the high-school units — digital life, broadcasting online, copyright, cyberbullying, online identity, community, privacy, college applications, hate speech, retouching reality, collective intelligence — and then the grade 6–8 units on searching, scams, creators' rights, media balance, and digital drama. Each unit closed with a *Digital Bytes* activity and an assessment.
