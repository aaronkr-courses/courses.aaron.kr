[Code.org **Computer Science Discoveries**](https://code.org/educate/csd) for middle-school and ESL students — an introductory course that treats computer science as a medium for creativity, communication, and problem solving.

From the surviving plans, the class finished **Unit 2 (Web Development)** with a personal portfolio, worked through **Unit 3 (Interactive Animations and Games)** in Game Lab — shapes, sprites, the draw loop, conditionals, input, velocity, collisions, functions — and designed their own game, then moved into **Unit 4 (The Design Process)**: user-centered design, paper prototypes, App Lab, and app presentations. A second group ran about a week ahead on the same units.
