The goal of this course is a solid foundation of computer knowledge — **computer literacy** — for every student. Computer A is a required high school class, and the concepts covered directly impact students' success at university and, later, in the job market.

By the end of the course, students will demonstrate:

- Knowledge of the basic components and peripherals of computers
- A deep, practical understanding of online safety and computer security (social networking, program installation, virus detection & removal, passwords)
- Functional knowledge of Google Drive and other Google tools
- Functional knowledge of the major office suites, online and offline
- A computer portfolio showcasing their skills and projects

**Section I — Knowledge:** touch typing · overview of computers (hardware, software, online, safety) · Google tools · office suites. Graded mainly with short knowledge quizzes.

**Section II — Application** (after midterms): students (1) write a research paper, (2) illustrate it with charts and graphs, (3) prepare and deliver a speech on it, (4) create supporting documents, and (5) submit everything as a final online portfolio in WordPress.

**Materials:** no textbook — worksheets and handouts are provided and shared through Google Drive. Students are encouraged to keep a keyword notebook (new terms may appear on tests). Fridays are catch-up & portfolio days.
