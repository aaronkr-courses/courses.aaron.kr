A foundation in the basics of **graphic design and publishing**. This elective introduces students to the real-world design problems and solutions that designers and companies deal with every day when marketing themselves and their products — and ends with the school yearbook.

By the end of the course, students will demonstrate:

- Knowledge of the basic components of graphic design: color, font choice and pairing, layout and whitespace, balance, and taking a publication from concept to print
- A practical understanding of branding and how companies use "brand power" in marketing
- Functional knowledge of MS Publisher and Adobe InDesign
- A design portfolio showcasing their skills and projects

**Section I — Knowledge:** design concepts (fonts, colors, images, backgrounds, shapes, icons, layout, whitespace, balance) and programs (MS Word, MS Publisher, Adobe InDesign), practiced with short design challenges in [Canva](https://www.canva.com/designschool/).

**Section II — Application** (after midterms): students (1) redesign a famous brand, (2) publish a blog and "market" it to classmates, (3) produce a series of school newsletters, and (4) design the final school yearbook.
