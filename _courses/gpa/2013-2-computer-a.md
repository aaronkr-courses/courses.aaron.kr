Students learn the basic use and function of computers for everyday, academic, and business purposes — building **English touch-typing** skills and then putting them to work in **Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Publisher**. Many students arrive with some experience in these programs; the goal is a fuller knowledge of each, and an understanding of how to use them effectively for school (and life).

Every project is published to a personal **WordPress** blog, which becomes the student's portfolio by the end of the semester.

**Course sections**

1. English keyboard skills and typing
2. MS Word — formatting, reports, resumes
3. MS Excel & money — personal and business finance
4. MS PowerPoint & speech
5. MS Publisher & graphic design
6. WordPress portfolios

**Materials:** all class materials are online; class time is given to work on the school computers.
