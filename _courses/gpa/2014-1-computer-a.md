Computer A in Spring 2014 was **team-taught** by three teachers — Aaron (MS Publisher), Olga (typing and PowerPoint / Google Presentation), and Ephraim (Google Drive, blogs, Word, and Excel / Google Spreadsheet). The main purpose of the class was simply to give students *a lot* of projects of increasing difficulty, so they learn each program well and are prepared for university-style projects (and later life).

Most of the projects were posted on the course blog at jjuenglish.com/compa.

**Materials:** class projects and handouts online; class time on the school computers.
